In short

This website sets no cookies for visitors and does not recognise you across days. For aggregated visitor statistics we use Vercel Web Analytics from our hosting provider, and we count how often the venue pages are opened (both see below). Fonts are loaded from our own server, not from third parties. There are two forms: the request on our own pages and the owner request on the venue pages and on namax.at/en/free-page (both see below).

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser type in order to deliver the page and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA the provider relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Visitor statistics (Vercel Web Analytics)

To see which pages are opened and where visitors come from, we use Vercel Web Analytics by Vercel Inc. (USA), our hosting provider. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It records the page opened, the page you came from, the country, and the browser, operating system and device type. To count visits, Vercel derives a check value (hash) from your request, which is discarded after 24 hours, so you are not recognised across days. We see only aggregated numbers, never individual people. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding and improving how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA, Vercel relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Request form

On our own pages (home page and services) you can send us a request. We store who you are (whether your business already has a website), what you need, your phone number, whether we should call or write on WhatsApp, your name and your message if you give them, and the time. We use these details only to contact you about your request and to handle it. The legal basis is your consent given by sending (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); you can withdraw your consent at any time. Handled requests are deleted 90 days after they were dealt with, unhandled ones after 180 days at the latest. As with the owner form, we count only, per page and day, which button opened the form, which steps and choices were reached, at which step it was closed and whether it was sent, with no name, phone number or text. If you choose WhatsApp, we write to you there; the privacy terms of WhatsApp then apply.

Contact by phone or e-mail

If you call us or send an e-mail, we likewise use your details only to handle your enquiry.

Visit counter on the venue pages

When you open a page name.namax.at, we count one view for that venue and that day. We store only the number of views per venue and day and roughly where the view came from (e.g. Google, a social network, direct): no IP address, no cookies and nothing that recognises you. For the form "Are you the owner?" we likewise count only, per venue and day, which button opened it, which steps and choices (who, what) were reached, at which step it was closed and whether it was sent, with no name, phone number or text. Only we and the owner of the venue see these numbers. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the pages are used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Owner request ("Are you the owner?")

On the venue pages (name.namax.at) and on namax.at/en/free-page owners and their staff can send a request. On the free page we also store which venue you chose, or the name and address you give for a venue that is not listed yet. The venue search itself stores nothing. We also store who you are (owner, family/staff or other), what you would like, your name, your phone number, whether we should call or write on WhatsApp, your message and the time. We use these details only to contact you about your request and to handle it. The legal basis is your consent given by sending (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time, e.g. by e-mail to the address above. Handled requests are deleted 90 days after they were dealt with, unhandled ones after 180 days at the latest. The data stays with our hosting and database provider and is not passed on.

Owner chat

When we open the chat for your page after talking to you, you can change opening hours, phone, prices and photos there. We handle opening hours, phone and the reply buttons in the chat ourselves, and photos for your page go straight to our storage. Only two things are processed by an AI service: text you write freely, and the photo of a price board if you want the prices read from it. This service is Claude by Anthropic PBC (USA); Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Standard contractual clauses apply to the transfer to the USA. The AI changes nothing by itself: changes only appear once you confirm them in the chat. We store messages and photos with our hosting and database provider for as long as you manage your page through us; on request we delete them earlier. If the page is deleted, its photos are deleted too. The legal basis is managing your page at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention

We delete enquiries once they are dealt with and no statutory retention duties apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise them, write to the address above. Complaints can be lodged with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: dsb.gv.at.
