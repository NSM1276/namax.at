In short

This website sets no cookies for visitors and uses no tracking or analytics tools. Fonts are loaded from our own server, not from third parties. The only form is the owner request on the venue pages (see below).

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser type in order to deliver the page and keep it secure. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and stable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the USA the provider relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Contact via WhatsApp or Telegram

The buttons on this website are plain links. Only when you tap one does WhatsApp or Telegram open; from then on the privacy terms of those services apply. We use what you write to us only to answer your enquiry and to carry out an order (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not pass this data on.

Contact by phone or e-mail

If you call us or send an e-mail, we likewise use your details only to handle your enquiry.

Owner request ("Are you the owner?")

On the venue pages (name.namax.at) owners and their staff can send a request. We store who you are (owner, family/staff or other), what you would like, your name, your phone number, whether we should call or write on WhatsApp, your message and the time. We use these details only to contact you about your request and to handle it. The legal basis is your consent given by sending (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You can withdraw your consent at any time, e.g. by e-mail to the address above. Handled requests are deleted 90 days after they were dealt with, unhandled ones after 180 days at the latest. The data stays with our hosting and database provider and is not passed on.

Owner chat

When we open the chat for your page after talking to you, you can change opening hours, phone, prices and photos there. To answer, your messages are processed automatically by an AI service: Claude by Anthropic PBC (USA). Anthropic receives the text of your messages and, if you send one, the photo of a price board; Anthropic does not use this data to train its models. Standard contractual clauses apply to the transfer to the USA. The AI changes nothing by itself: changes only appear once you confirm them in the chat. We store messages and photos with our hosting and database provider for as long as you manage your page through us; on request we delete them earlier. If the page is deleted, its photos are deleted too. The legal basis is managing your page at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Retention

We delete enquiries once they are dealt with and no statutory retention duties apply.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise them, write to the address above. Complaints can be lodged with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: dsb.gv.at.
